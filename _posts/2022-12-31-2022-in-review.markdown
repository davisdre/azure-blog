As I'm sitting here at home and thinking of the past year I thought I would create a 2022 in review. Thought I would share my thoughts of the year. 

- Azure/AWS fundamental certifications help you establish a foundation in Cloud Computing and help you gear the skills/roles you are interested in.
- Have come to find out that I really have a passion/love for DevOps. Love it when I'm able to do DevOps-ish items.
- A Cloud Guru has come to be my primary source place for gaining/learning new Cloud Skills. Their content talks to me.
- Tackling multi-cloud can be a handful and challenging, because sometimes it can take the mind the time to switch back and forth on the name of the services you are needing to use.
- Visual Studio Code, has become more of a useful tool for scripting, IaC, and more.

I've learn alot this 2022 and hope to continue onward in 2023 as well. 

Happy New Years!
